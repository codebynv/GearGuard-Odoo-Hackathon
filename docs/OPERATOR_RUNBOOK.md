# Operator Runbook

## Daily checks

- Review open maintenance requests.
- Identify critical and overdue work.
- Confirm equipment status matches active work.
- Review preventive maintenance items coming due.
- Check for failed scheduled jobs.

## When something looks wrong

1. Open the affected equipment and request.
2. Check the current state and recent changes.
3. Confirm whether the transition is valid.
4. Record the issue and relevant non-sensitive details.
5. Escalate persistent failures to the maintainer.
